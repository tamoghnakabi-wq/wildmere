Fixes floating rocks and bushes in the sky.

### Fixed
- Decoration props (boulders, small rocks, bushes, logs, mushrooms) from the main menu's background world, or from a previously loaded world, kept being drawn after you started or loaded a world, so they floated in the air above your terrain. Each world's props are now removed when that world closes.
- Includes v0.1.1's fixes: clouds no longer look multi-layered, and held plants are drawn at a natural size.

### Known issues
- macOS builds are ad-hoc signed, not notarized: approve the first launch in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
- Windows builds are not code-signed: SmartScreen asks for "More info › Run anyway".
- Windows and Intel Macs have only passed automated checks on GitHub's test machines; the game was played and benchmarked on an Apple M4 MacBook Air.
