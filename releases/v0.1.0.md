The first public build of Wildmere: an endless, seeded voxel world to explore, mine and build in, with weather, wildlife and a full day/night cycle.

### New
- Procedurally generated worlds from a seed: temperate and dark forests, snowy mountains up to y≈230, desert dunes and mesas, flower plains, rivers, lakes and coasts, with smooth biome transitions.
- Caves (caverns and tunnels), coal, iron and gold ore veins, and hand-made decoration models (boulders, logs, mushrooms, ice spikes, sandstone pillars).
- Mining with timed breaking and tools (wood/stone/iron pickaxe, axe and shovel) with durability; collision-safe building.
- 36-slot inventory with drag and drop, and recipe crafting with workbench and furnace stations.
- Day/night cycle, dynamic weather, procedural clouds, water with reflections and foam, original HD block textures.
- Deer, rabbits and songbirds that graze, wander and flee; real-recording ambience, footsteps and music.
- Save and continue any time (autosave every two minutes); LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/ULTRA graphics presets, key rebinding, audio mixing.

### Known issues
- macOS builds are ad-hoc signed, not notarized: approve the first launch in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
- Windows builds are not code-signed: SmartScreen asks for "More info › Run anyway".
- Windows and Intel Macs have only passed automated checks on GitHub's test machines; the game was played and benchmarked on an Apple M4 MacBook Air.
- No multiplayer, hostile creatures or hunger yet.
