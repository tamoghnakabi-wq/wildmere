A visual fix release: cleaner skies and better-sized held items.

### Fixed
- Clouds no longer look multi-layered. The sky drew several offset copies of each cloud (a sliced, stacked look, worst near the horizon); every cloud now appears once, shaped by its lighting, with ground cloud-shadows and water reflections matching.
- Held flowers, mushrooms and grass were drawn far too large in first person; they now sit at a natural size.

### Known issues
- macOS builds are ad-hoc signed, not notarized: approve the first launch in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
- Windows builds are not code-signed: SmartScreen asks for "More info › Run anyway".
- Windows and Intel Macs have only passed automated checks on GitHub's test machines; the game was played and benchmarked on an Apple M4 MacBook Air.
