Fullscreen now fills your whole screen instead of showing black bars above and below the game.

### Fixed
- **Fullscreen and borderless modes use your display's own resolution.** Before, they kept the 16:9 window size, so on 16:10 screens such as MacBooks the picture was letterboxed. On MacBooks with a camera notch, macOS still keeps the thin strip beside the notch black, as it does for every fullscreen app.
- **The 3D view stays fast on high-resolution screens.** At 100% render scale it now renders at about 1920 × 1200 and scales up, while menus and text stay at full sharpness. Raise "Render scale" in Settings › Graphics for a sharper picture, or lower it for more speed.
- **Switching to windowed mode gives a usable window**, about three-quarters of the screen and centered, instead of a small one in the corner.

### Updating
- If you have 0.1.3, Wildmere downloads this update by itself and installs it when you quit (or use "Restart to update" on the title screen).
- If you have 0.1.0–0.1.2, download this version from the website once; later updates are automatic.

### Known issues
- macOS builds are ad-hoc signed, not notarized: approve the first launch in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
- Windows builds are not code-signed: SmartScreen asks for "More info › Run anyway".
- Windows and Intel Macs have only passed automated checks on GitHub's test machines; the game was played and benchmarked on an Apple M4 MacBook Air.
